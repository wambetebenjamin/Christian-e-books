WELCOME

Learning to pray from the heart of God

There are things we do not know how to pray for except by the Spirit of God. Prayer is more than presenting urgent needs; it is learning the heart of the Father.

“The Spirit Himself makes intercession for us…”Romans 8:26

Move through this booklet slowly. Read, listen, respond, and allow the Word to lead you into the perfect will of God.

PART ONE · THE MESSAGE

What is the will of God?

God meets us at our point of need, but He also calls us beyond the demands of the flesh and into His purpose.

01

Not every desire is direction

Most of our needs are not met because they are shaped by the flesh rather than by the will of God. An emergency may be urgent to us, but it may not be God’s appointed moment.

02

Pray in the Spirit

The Spirit knows the mind of God and the perfect will of God for every individual. As we become effective in prayer, we become sensitive to His direction.

03

His will starts small

The will of God may begin like a mustard seed. Say yes to the next instruction. Faithfulness in the small opens the way for the fullness of His purpose.

A PICTURE OF SURRENDER

Follow the light you have been given.

PART TWO · THREE COURTS OF SURRENDER

From the outer court to His presence

01

Outer Court

Obedience begins. We bring our lives honestly before God.

02

Holy Place

Fellowship deepens. The Word and prayer shape our desires.

03

Holy of Holies

His presence becomes our priority. His will becomes our delight.

“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”Psalm 119:105

PART THREE · HOW TO FIND HIS WILL

By the Word of God

The Word shines on your path. Let it dwell in you richly. When we refuse to seek Scripture, we can pray sincerely and still pray amiss.

Walking in the Spirit means following the direction He has given. Leadership is critical because submitted leadership helps give direction and protect the flock.

Daniel prayed, and an angel was sent. Effective prayer is prayer aligned with heaven.

PART FOUR · PRAYER FOCUS

Bring my heart into Your will

Father, teach me to pray in the Spirit, recognize Your voice, and obey Your Word. Let Your will become my greatest desire.

“My will is ultimate because I was created by God and for God.”

PART FIVE · PERSONAL RESPONSE

Make it personal

Write a prayer, a decision, or one small act of obedience God is placing before you.
